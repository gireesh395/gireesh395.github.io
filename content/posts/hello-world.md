I've been building a bunch of stuff lately — homelab clusters, security automation, CI/CD pipelines — and most of it has just been sitting in private repos where nobody (including future me) can easily see what I actually did or why.

This blog is where that changes. The plan is simple: whenever I finish a project or learn something worth remembering, I write it up here. That includes:

- Homelab and infrastructure projects (Kubernetes, reverse proxies, VMs)
- Security engineering work (SOAR pipelines, CI/CD hardening, secret scanning)
- Cryptography and coursework labs
- Whatever else turns out to be worth documenting

If you're reading this: welcome. More posts coming soon.
